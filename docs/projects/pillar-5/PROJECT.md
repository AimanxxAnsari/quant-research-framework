# Pillar 5: how often do quant methods fool you?

## Abstract
We simulate data with known parameters and measure how standard quant tools behave at realistic
sample sizes. Results (regenerated by `qrf study pillar5`, in the README): one year of data
underestimates a 10-day mean-reversion half-life (median 8.2 days, 73% of estimates too fast);
naive regressions of unrelated random walks are "significant" 90% of the time, while the
Engle-Granger test stays below its 5% size; screening 190 unrelated pairs still yields about 10
false "cointegrated" pairs; under volatility clustering the homoskedastic variance-ratio test
rejects a true random walk 25% of the time versus 5.6% for the robust version; a normal-quantile
99% VaR on fat-tailed returns breaches 1.9% of days and fails Kupiec every time; and the best of
100 no-skill strategies shows a 10-year Sharpe of 0.78, matching the Bailey & Lopez de Prado
expected-maximum formula, while the best of a 25-rule MA grid on a random walk scores 0.41 in
sample and -0.07 out of sample.

## 1. Why
Later pillars estimate half-lives (pairs), screen for cointegration, test random walks, forecast
VaR and select strategies. Each has a known way to mislead. Measuring those failure rates first
tells us how to read every later result.

## 2. Method
Design: [validation](../../design/validation.md); decision:
[ADR-0006](../../decisions/ADR-0006-validate-on-synthetic-data-first.md). Fixed seeds;
400-500 repetitions per cell (VaR: 100 paths of 2,500 days; overfitting: 300 repetitions per
trial count).

## 3. Results
See [README, Pillar 5 results](../../../README.md#pillar-5-results).

## 4. Implications for later pillars
- **Pairs (P6):** report half-life uncertainty, not a point estimate; report the number of pairs
  screened; expect about 5% false discoveries at a 5% level.
- **Backtesting (P4):** log every trial; benchmark the best Sharpe against E[max] (Deflated
  Sharpe Ratio); always test the selected rule out of sample.
- **Random-walk tests (P2):** use heteroskedasticity-robust statistics.
- **Risk (P9):** normal-quantile VaR under-reserves on fat tails; test with Kupiec and
  conditional coverage.

## 5. Limitations
- Generators are stylised (single-regime GARCH-t, Gaussian OU spreads).
- Repetition counts give standard errors of about 1 percentage point on rejection rates.
